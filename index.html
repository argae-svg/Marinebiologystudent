Published research in marine conservation

This website is designed to help marine biology students quickly access published scientific literature. Each entry below identifies the publication, its research focus, and how it can help students understand marine conservation.

Marine biodiversity • 2026

Gaps and drivers of global marine animal biodiversity from the surface to abyss

Author: Hanieh Saeedi

Journal: Nature Communications, 17, Article 4553 (2026).

Student focus: Shows how global biodiversity databases can be used to study marine species richness, sampling gaps, depth, environmental drivers, and human impacts.

How students can use this library

Use the publications to build background knowledge, identify research gaps, compare conservation approaches, support literature reviews, and locate original scientific evidence. Always read the original publication before citing a finding in an academic paper.

Methodology

Field-to-dataset workflow

A transparent workflow makes marine research reproducible and easier to evaluate.

01 • SITE SELECTION

Study areas

Select representative coastal sites and record habitat characteristics.

02 • FIELD SAMPLING

Observation

Conduct standardized transects, quadrats, visual surveys, or specimen documentation.

03 • IDENTIFICATION

Taxonomy

Identify organisms using appropriate taxonomic references and verification procedures.

04 • ANALYSIS

Biodiversity metrics

Calculate richness, abundance, diversity indices, and compare sites as appropriate.
