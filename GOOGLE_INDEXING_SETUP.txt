GOOGLE INDEXING SETUP — MARINE BIOLOGY RESEARCH PORTAL

1. Publish the site with GitHub Pages.
2. Replace YOUR-USERNAME in:
   - index.html
   - robots.txt
   - sitemap.xml
   with your actual GitHub username.
3. Push/upload all three files:
   index.html
   robots.txt
   sitemap.xml
4. Open Google Search Console:
   https://search.google.com/search-console
5. Add your GitHub Pages URL as a URL-prefix property.
6. Verify ownership using one of Google's available verification methods.
7. Open URL Inspection in Search Console and enter your homepage URL.
8. If Google can access it, request indexing.
9. In Search Console, open Sitemaps and submit:
   sitemap.xml
10. Keep the website publicly accessible and do not add a noindex tag.

Google may take several days or longer to crawl and index a newly published site.
Indexing is not guaranteed immediately, and appearing in Search is separate from
simply having a public website.

Google guidance:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/search-console-start
